ABOUT JBL

Born from a shared dream

Joyful Bilingual Learning Academy began with a simple idea:

Let children learn languages through stories, explore the world through play, and develop imagination through reading.

We believe every child learns differently.

Our role is to help children develop language, confidence, curiosity and a genuine love of learning.

OUR FOUNDER

Dr Kau Kun Chui

Founder

Dr Kau Kun Chui studied in Singapore and Hong Kong and later taught at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. She also served as an Assistant Professor at Xiamen University Malaysia.

Her experience spans language education, university teaching, early childhood education and community education.

While teaching at university, she worked closely with students who were struggling academically and helped them gradually improve and complete their studies.

That experience shaped one of her strongest beliefs:

Education is not only about helping the strongest students move ahead. It is also about helping those who need more support to move forward.
